# Using Search boxes on the Portal
Most Search boxes on the Portal are standardised, so this advice should apply everywhere.

In general, searches are split into individual terms. For example, if you search for `red and blue`, you will get results for `red`, `and`, and `blue`. You can use quotes to force a string to stay together. For example, `"red and blue"` will not be split into multiple terms. However, multiple terms *with* strings will be searched together. For example, `"red and blue" green` will show you results with `red and blue` OR `green`.
